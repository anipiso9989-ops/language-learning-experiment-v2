This is my 2nd attempt at learning languages. 

There are 4 major differences with this system:
- Self Built. Not as much reliance on external resources
- Flexible. The load is less, and that allows you to allocate time more easily to language learning
- Simple. Goes with my 2nd point; the load may be less, but the quality of it is higher. Also, it's easier to actually record everything (in v1 I had to record everything daily, weekly, and monthly; now there's only session by session observations).
- Free! In my v1 system, I had to pay $15 monthly. This system is completely free.

I'm also not going to do weekly and monthly analyses, as I've found they add no extra value; I'm keeping session-by-session observations though.
